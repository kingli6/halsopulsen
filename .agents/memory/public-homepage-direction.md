---
name: Public homepage direction
description: What the public HälsoPulsen landing page should and should not promote while the tracker is being built.
---

The homepage should sell the current practice services and booking path, not
the unfinished private tracker or the retired group-challenge concept. The
tracker can return later as a truthful visual example of logging a workout and
using history/statistics.

**Why:** Visitors currently arrive to book massage, nutrition coaching, or
personal training. Promoting an admin route or public competition creates
expectations that the product and access model do not yet support.

**How to apply:** Keep the hero, services, practice explanation, qualifications,
and contact path. Treat progress counters, workout tools, and the external form
as candidates for simplification or removal when they do not help bookings or
when their maintenance/anti-spam cost outweighs their value.
