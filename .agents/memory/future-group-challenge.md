---
name: Future group challenge
description: The direction for preserving and eventually rebuilding the old group challenge as a later product mode.
---

Keep the old group challenge out of public navigation and do not reuse its
obsolete implementation as a production foundation. If the idea returns, build
a challenge mode on top of the individual tracker rather than extending the old
open leaderboard/chat prototype.

**Why:** The concept is appealing, but the old version lacks the privacy,
moderation, rules, tie handling, time-zone behavior, and reliable participant
model needed for real use.

**How to apply:** First finish the private individual planning and logging
loop. Then ask what users actually want to compare, define a small challenge
with explicit dates/rules/privacy, keep personal logs separate from rankings,
and make chat and AI optional.
