---
name: Critical product decisions
description: The product and UX decision rule to challenge weak ideas and prefer proven, simple patterns.
---

Be constructively critical of product and design ideas. Prefer familiar patterns that already work in task, calendar, and training products; only invent a new interaction when it solves a real problem better. Avoid overengineering the MVP.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants honest critique, wants to avoid reinventing the wheel, and prefers using proven solutions from established products such as Google-style task and calendar flows when they fit.

**How to apply:** Start with the user's primary job, compare the proposed flow with conventional products, call out weak hierarchy or unnecessary features plainly, and choose the smallest familiar solution that supports the real behavior.
